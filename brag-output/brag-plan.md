# Brag Plan: صدقة — Sadaqa Quran Radio

## What is this app?
Sadaqa is an Arabic Quran radio that never stops: anyone creates a dedication link
(صدقة or هدية) in a loved one's name, and everyone who opens that link joins the *same
live recitation at the same moment* — one global broadcast, computed by pure math, with
rewards that keep counting.

## The angle
A calm, reverent 20-second **tutorial** in Arabic (RTL): exactly how you create a link,
and exactly what happens when someone opens it. The video must feel like the product —
warm parchment, emerald green, Amiri calligraphy — and must show the real flow:
form → button → link → live radio page. The emotional payoff is صدقة جارية: a gift that
keeps reciting forever.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The crescent brand mark of the site draws itself in over the parchment background while
the bismillah line (بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ) appears, then the site's
actual headline slams in: **إذاعة قرآنية لا تتوقف** — with a subtitle
"أنشئ رابطًا باسم من تحب". Quiet, confident, unmistakably this app.

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **The real create form, filled by a simulated cursor** — the صدقة/هدية pill tapped,
   لمن الصدقة typed with "أمي" (RTL text typed letter-group by letter-group), reciter
   pills confirmed, then the green **أنشئ الرابط** button pressed.
2. **The success banner** — "الرابط جاهز — شاركه" with the real URL shape
   `sadaqa.app/streaming/1835482`, a cursor click on **نسخ** (copy chirp), and the link
   chip flying off to share.
3. **What the link does** — the stream page: dedication card "صدقة عن أمي", the pulsing
   **مباشر** chip, play pressed, progress bar filling, reciter name, and the
   ختمة counter ticking up — while 3 small device cards light up one by one, all hearing
   the same second.

## Outro / punchline
Brand lockup: crescent + **صدقة** with the closing line
"رابطٌ واحد · تلاوةٌ لا تتوقف · أجرٌ لا ينقطع" and the bare route `/streaming/1835482`
as the final beat. Gentle end chime, fade to parchment.

## User flow worth showing
Open form → choose صدقة + type names + confirm reciters → أنشئ الرابط → success banner
with copyable link → open link → live radio page (dedication card, مباشر chip, play,
khatma counter) → share via WhatsApp. This flow IS the video: entry → key action → result.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: "a serene how-to that feels like a sadaqa invitation — quiet confidence, no noise"
- Interpretation: Fewer scenes, longer holds, generous negative space; motion is soft and
  exact; text is set in the product's own fonts; the restraint matches the reverence of the subject.

## Duration: 20 seconds (user-specified)

## Audio direction
- Role: **pure SFX only — the user explicitly disabled music AND voice.** No music bed, no
  narration, no TTS. Silence between cues is part of the design.
- Music: none (disabled by user request)
- Music treatment: n/a
- Music cue guidance: n/a — no music; SFX timed to visual events, not beats
- Audio-reactive treatment: none
- SFX posture: sparse-to-moderate, motion-matched, professional restraint — every cue
  maps to a real interaction (pill tap, keystroke, button press, copy chirp, link whoosh,
  play pop, counter tick, soft end chime)
- Audio-coupled moments: pill tap on صدقة; typed text in لمن الصدقة with soft key ticks;
  button press; URL reveal tick; copy click; link fly-off whoosh; مباشر chip pulse blip;
  play pop; progress ticks; khatma counter increments; device cards lighting one by one
- Restraint rule: no music, no voice, nothing loud or playful; each SFX is short, soft,
  and earned by a visible action — never decorative

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook / brand — 3s
Parchment-to-night backdrop. The crescent mark draws in; بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ
ٱلرَّحِيمِ in Amiri Quran settles above; headline **إذاعة قرآنية لا تتوقف** lands fast
then holds (needs ≥1.6s settled); subtitle "أنشئ رابطًا باسم من تحب" fades in under it.
Sequential/interaction: none — one composed reveal.
Audio intent: a single warm, deep, soft hit plus a faint shimmer — reverent, not dramatic.
Audio-coupled idea: soft low swell on the headline land; tiny sparkle on the bismillah settle.
Music: none (disabled).
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Fill the form — 5s
The real create form rebuilt in HTML (RTL): panel on parchment. Cursor taps the
**صدقة** radio pill (tap). Field **لمن الصدقة** types "أمي" (key ticks, RTL). Reciter
pills row confirms — a few pills pulse once each (soft ticks). Cursor presses the green
**أنشئ الرابط** button (press + confirm chirp).
Sequential/interaction: yes — pill tap → typed text → pill pulse → button press, in order.
Audio intent: intimate, tactile, minimal — every sound is a finger touching glass.
Audio-coupled idea: key ticks during typing; distinct click on pill and button.
Music: none (disabled).
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — The link is ready — 4s
Success banner from the real product: **الرابط جاهز — شاركه** and the URL
`sadaqa.app/streaming/1835482` reveals left-to-right (RTL-safe: reveals toward the left
as read in Arabic) with soft ticks. Cursor clicks **نسخ** — chip flashes "نُسخ ✓"-style
confirmation, then the link chip flies off-screen (whoosh) toward the share moment.
Sequential/interaction: yes — URL reveal → copy click → chip fly-off.
Audio intent: bright, satisfying confirmation — the "gift wrapped" moment.
Audio-coupled idea: tick per URL chunk; copy chirp; whoosh on fly-off.
Music: none (disabled).
Transition mood: clean whoosh → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — What the link does — 5s
The stream page rebuilt: dedication card "صدقة عن أمي" with message «رحمةً ونورًا»,
sender "من أحمد بمحبة". The **مباشر** chip pulses (blip). Cursor presses play (pop);
progress bar starts filling; reciter name "مشاري العفاسي" and ختمة counter tick up
(2–3 increments). Three small device cards (هاتف · حاسوب · جوال) light up one by one —
all hearing the same second of the same surah.
Sequential/interaction: yes — chip pulse → play press → counter ticks → device cards in order.
Audio intent: alive and warm — the radio switching on, then a quiet sense of many people together.
Audio-coupled idea: play pop, progress ticks, counter ticks, one soft blip per device card.
Music: none (disabled).
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — Outro / brand lockup — 3s
Night background returns. Crescent + **صدقة** lockup; closing line
"رابطٌ واحد · تلاوةٌ لا تتوقف · أجرٌ لا ينقطع" rises in; below it the route
`/streaming/1835482` sits quietly as the last readable beat. Gentle fade to end.
Sequential/interaction: none — one lockup reveal.
Audio intent: resolution — one soft chime and silence.
Audio-coupled idea: single soft chime on the lockup settle.
Music: none (disabled).
Transition mood: fade out — end.

**Music mood for this video:** none (music disabled by user)
**Audio summary:** pure SFX, sparse and reverent — a warm hit on the hook, tactile glass
taps through the form, a satisfying copy-chirp and whoosh at the link, a play-pop and
counter ticks on the stream page, and one soft chime at the lockup.
